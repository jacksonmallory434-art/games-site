Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

YourGamePlug does not ask for accounts, names, emails, or any personal information, and does not use its own tracking cookies.

All games are hosted on this site. Some games save your progress or high score in your own browser so you can pick up where you left off; that data stays on your device.

The Retro Player opens game files in your browser; files you choose are never uploaded to this site. To run, it downloads emulator components from EmulatorJS's server (cdn.emulatorjs.org).
