Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

MathAdvice does not ask for accounts, names, emails, or any personal information, and does not use its own tracking cookies.

All games are hosted on this site. Some games save your best scores, and the site saves your light/dark setting, in your own browser. That data stays on your device, and you can clear your scores on the Settings page.
