Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

What MathAdvice collects

Nothing personal. MathAdvice has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, or any other personal information.

Some games save your best scores, and the site saves your light/dark setting, in your own browser. That data stays on your device, and you can clear your scores on the Settings page.

Advertising

MathAdvice shows ads through Google AdSense to keep the site free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure how they perform.

Because many players are students, MathAdvice asks Google to treat the site as directed to children. In this mode, Google limits data collection and shows non-personalized ads, which are based on the page's content rather than your browsing history.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent before cookies are used for ads.
