Tangent Surfer

Calculus All games

About this game

Surf a curve at night. Keep your board on the tangent line by matching the slope of the wave, its derivative, at every moment.

How to play

Move your mouse or finger up and down, or hold ↑ and ↓, to tilt the board. Stay close to the wave's slope to keep your balance. Within 3° scores Perfect points.
