Times Table Tower

Arithmetic All games

About this game

Build the tallest tower you can, one multiplication fact at a time. Every right answer drops a new block onto the stack, but answer wrong or too slowly and the top block tumbles off. The timer gets shorter and the facts get bigger as your tower grows.

How to play

Tap or click the right answer, or press 1 to 4. You have 3 lives, and each wrong or late answer costs one.
