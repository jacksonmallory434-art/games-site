Mean Machine

Statistics All games

About this game

A claw drops number balls onto a balance beam. The beam only balances when the mean of the balls sits exactly on the pivot.

How to play

Tap or press Space to drop a ball on the number under the claw. Use every drop to make the mean match the pivot. If it tips, you lose a life.
