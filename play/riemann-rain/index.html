Riemann Rain

Calculus All games

About this game

Paint rain fills the area under a curve one rectangle at a time. Stop each one at the right height to build an accurate Riemann sum.

How to play

Tap or press Space to stop the rising paint. Paint above the curve is spill and empty space below it is a gap. Keep both small across six curves.
