Vector Pilot

Precalculus All games

About this game

Fly a spaceship by adding vectors. Each turn your thrust is added to your velocity, and your velocity moves the ship. Dock gently at the station.

How to play

Pick one of the 9 glowing dots (or press Q W E / A S D / Z X C) to add thrust. Land on the station with a slow speed. Avoid asteroids.
