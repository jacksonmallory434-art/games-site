Pour It

Geometry All games

About this game

Pour water into the tank it fills exactly. Work out the volumes of cylinders, cones, spheres, boxes, pyramids and prisms, then watch it fill, spill over or come up short.

How to play

Drag a jug onto the tank that holds exactly the same volume (or tap the jug, then the tank; keys: arrows pick a jug, 1-4 pour). Use V = πr²h, ⅓πr²h, ⁴⁄₃πr³, l·w·h, ⅓·l·w·h and ½·b·h·length. A spill or a short pour costs a heart. Later levels have target lines, several jugs to combine and chain pours.
