Orbit Shot

Precalculus All games

About this game

Slingshot a probe around a star. Gravity bends its path into a conic section: a circle, an ellipse, a parabola, or a hyperbola. Thread every ring in one shot.

How to play

Drag back and release to launch. Watch the readout for the orbit's type and eccentricity. Fly through every ring and avoid the moons and the sun.
