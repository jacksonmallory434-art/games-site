Complex Maze

Precalculus All games

About this game

Your ship is a complex number. Adding slides it across the complex plane, and multiplying by i spins it around the origin. Reach the star while dodging black holes.

How to play

Use the move buttons (or number keys) to change z. Hover a move to preview it. Reach the star in as few moves as you can.
