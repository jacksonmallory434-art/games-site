Dice Derby

Statistics All games

About this game

Eleven racers, two dice. Each roll moves the racer whose number matches the sum. Bet on the racers you think will win, and look for good value in the odds.

How to play

Tap a lane to bet 10 chips on that racer, then start the race. Odds change every race. Finish 8 races with as many chips as you can.
