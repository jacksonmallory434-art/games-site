Shape Shifter

Geometry All games

About this game

Slide, turn and flip a shape on the coordinate grid until it fits its outline. Each move shows its rule, like (x, y) → (x + 3, y − 1). Solve each puzzle in as few moves as you can.

How to play

Pick a tool, then click a grid point. Slide moves the glowing corner to that point. Turn rotates 90° or 180° about that point. Flip reflects over x = k, y = k, y = x or y = −x. A ghost shows where the shape will land. Walls block the path. Match par for 3 stars. Undo and Reset are always available. Keys 1–8 pick tools, arrow keys and Enter aim and apply, U undoes, R resets.
