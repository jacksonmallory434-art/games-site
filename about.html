About MathAdvice

MathAdvice is a free collection of math practice games. Each game is built around a math skill, from mental arithmetic and times tables up through algebra, geometry, statistics, precalculus, and an introduction to calculus, and turns it into something quick and fun to play.

Everything runs in the browser with nothing to download and no accounts. MathAdvice is free because it shows ads from Google, kept outside the game area. Ads are set to child-directed mode, so they aren't personalized.

For school networks:

  • All games are hosted on this site and nothing loads from outside game sites. The only outside service is Google AdSense, which shows ads outside the game area.
  • No sign-ups, and MathAdvice itself collects no personal information. High scores are saved only in the player's own browser. See the privacy policy for how ads work.
  • Games work with a keyboard, mouse, or touchscreen, including Chromebooks.

Who makes it
