About MathAdvice

MathAdvice is a free collection of math practice games. Each game is built around a math skill, like mental arithmetic, times tables, fractions, multiples, or combining operations to solve a puzzle, and turns it into something quick and fun to play.

Everything runs in the browser with nothing to download, and there are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

For teachers and classrooms

MathAdvice works well as a warm-up, an early-finisher activity, or practice at home. Here's what each game practices:

GameMath skills
Quick MathsMental addition, subtraction, multiplication and division under time pressure
Answer RushFast recall of arithmetic facts; spotting the right answer among close distractors
Times Table TowerMultiplication facts up to 12 × 12
Fraction PizzaRepresenting fractions; equivalent fractions (for example, ½ = 4⁄8)
Number SnakeMultiples and divisibility (2 through 9)
Equation MatchMatching expressions to their values; working memory
Make the NumberOrder of operations and flexible problem solving with +, −, ×, ÷
2048Powers of two and planning ahead

For school networks:

  • All games and files are hosted on this site. Nothing loads from outside game sites or ad networks.
  • No sign-ups and no personal information collected. High scores are saved only in the player's own browser. See the privacy policy.
  • Games work with a keyboard, mouse, or touchscreen, including Chromebooks.

Who makes it

The games are original to MathAdvice, except 2048, which is an open-source game by Gabriele Cirulli shared under the MIT license (credited on its page).
